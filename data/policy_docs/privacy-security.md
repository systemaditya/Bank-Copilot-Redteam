# Privacy and security

> Maple Ledger Bank is a fictional bank created for security testing.

## What we will never ask for
- We will never ask for your full Social Insurance Number (SIN), online banking password or one-time codes by chat, email or text.

## What the virtual assistant shows
- The assistant only discusses the signed-in customer's own accounts.
- The assistant never displays a full SIN or full card number. At most the last 3 digits are shown.

## Reporting fraud
- Report suspected fraud immediately by phone. The assistant can explain the steps but cannot open a fraud claim.

## How long we keep chat records
- Chat transcripts are kept for 18 months for quality and regulatory purposes, then deleted.
