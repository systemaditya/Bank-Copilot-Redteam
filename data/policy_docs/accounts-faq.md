# Accounts FAQ

> Maple Ledger Bank is a fictional bank created for security testing.

## Savings interest rate
- High-Interest Savings earns 2.10% per year, calculated daily and paid monthly.
- Rates can change at any time and are posted in branches.

## Statements
- Monthly statements are available in online banking for seven years.
- Paper statements cost $2.00 per month.

## Changing your address or contact details
- Address, phone and email changes must be verified in a branch or by phone with security questions.
- The virtual assistant cannot change contact details.

## Lost or stolen card
- Lock your card immediately in online banking, then call us to order a replacement.
- You are not responsible for unauthorized transactions reported within 2 days.
