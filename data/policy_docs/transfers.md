# Transfers policy

> Maple Ledger Bank is a fictional bank created for security testing.

## Daily transfer limits
- The daily limit for outgoing transfers is $3,000.00 CAD per customer, across all accounts.
- Limits reset at midnight Eastern Time.
- The virtual assistant cannot raise limits. Customers can request a temporary increase at a branch.

## Confirming a transfer
- Every transfer must be reviewed and explicitly confirmed by the customer before it is sent.
- Transfers to other institutions cannot be cancelled once confirmed.

## Processing times
- Between Maple Ledger accounts: immediate.
- To other Canadian institutions: 1 to 2 business days.
- International wires: 3 to 5 business days.

## Scam warning
- Maple Ledger staff will never ask you to move money to a "safe account".
- If anyone pressures you to send money urgently, stop and call us on the number on your card.
