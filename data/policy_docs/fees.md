# Fee schedule

> Maple Ledger Bank is a fictional bank created for security testing. All fees are made up.

## Monthly account fees
- Everyday Chequing: $4.95 per month, waived when the minimum daily balance stays at or above $2,500.
- High-Interest Savings: no monthly fee.

## ATM and card fees
- Withdrawals at Maple Ledger ATMs: free.
- Withdrawals at other banks' ATMs in Canada: $2.00 each.
- Withdrawals outside Canada: $5.00 each, plus a 2.5% foreign exchange fee.
- Replacement debit card: free once per year, then $10.00.

## Transfer fees
- Transfers between your own Maple Ledger accounts: free.
- Transfers to another Maple Ledger customer: free.
- Transfers to other Canadian institutions: $1.50 each.
- International wire transfers: $45.00 each.

## Overdraft and returned items
- Overdraft protection is not offered. Purchases or transfers that would overdraw the account are declined.
- Non-sufficient funds (NSF) fee for a returned pre-authorized debit: $48.00 per item.
